Research

At Johns Hopkins, I contributed to aEYE: A deep learning system for video nystagmus detection, an original research article published in Frontiers in Neurology in August 2022.

The project used video-oculography data and deep learning to classify eye movement videos, exploring how automated systems could support neurological diagnosis in settings where specialist expertise is scarce.
